The P&G answer framework

Use the three elements in the guidance P&G provided. In most answers, spend the most time on what you did and why.

01 — Context

Explain the situation and what you needed to achieve.

02 — Actions

Describe your own decisions, steps and reasoning. Why did you choose that approach?

03 — Results

Explain what happened, its impact and what you learned. Be precise; don't invent outcomes.
